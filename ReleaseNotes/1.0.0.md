## Features

- **Find the key** of the song that plays on the Mac with `Control-Command-M`
- **Change the key** of the song at the same tempo, with a crossfade between keys
- **Lock Key** moves every next song to the same key
- **Keys remembered by song**, so a song that plays again starts on its key
- **The key of the song in the menu bar**, with the pitch change applied, and a turntable while the key isn't known
- **Always listening** finds the key of each song that starts playing, without the HUD
- **Lyrics** that scroll along with the song, from LRCLIB (`L`)
- **Karaoke** with the lyrics large and the vocals muted (`Return`), and `V` to mute the vocals in any layout
- **Play or pause** the song from the HUD with `Space`, with the tonearm lifting off the record
- Drag the HUD by its background to move it
- Lyrics without times get timed by listening to the song, with Apple's speech model on the Mac
- **Add lyrics** for a song that LRCLIB doesn't have, as plain text or LRC
- **Lyrics by Whisper** for a song that LRCLIB doesn't have, from Whisper on the Mac or on a server, with the song 30 seconds behind so each line shows before it is sung
- Whisper hears each part of a song once: what it wrote is saved as it comes in, and a later play waits only for the parts that it missed
- **Separate instruments and vocals** under the keys: mute the vocals, drums, bass, guitar or piano (`1` to `6`), or `Option`-click one to hear only that part, in any key
- **Winds** stem with a second model, for the sax, flute, duduk or trumpet in a song (`7`)
- Keys a fourth, a fifth or a whole step away are highlighted, like on the Camelot wheel
- **Pick a key with the keyboard**: `A` to `G` in the mode of the song, and `Option` with a letter in the other mode
- Hold `Option` to flip every key to the other mode, and `Option`-click to pick a key in that mode
- Two layouts: the wide one with a row of keys, and a card with the keys on a circle of fifths around the record (`S`)
- Settings in a sidebar, with pictures for the layout, the relative keys and the menu bar icon
