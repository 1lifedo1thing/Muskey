## Features

- **Microphone recording** to find the key of music played in the room, without the talk and noise around it (`⌃⌥⌘M`, or **Microphone** in the HUD)

![microphone recording](https://files.lowtechguys.com/changelog/muskey/1.4.0-microphone.png)

- **Hold keys**: hold a number key to play only that instrument, or a letter to pick that key in the other mode (major or minor)
    > Hold `W` for one waveform per instrument, and hold `R` to loop the whole song
- **Original volume**: press an instrument's number twice or double-click it to play it at 100% again
- **Volume ring** around an instrument that plays louder or quieter than the original
- **Mini player fade**: tap `fn` or three fingers over the mini player to fade it and click the windows under it
    > The key can be changed in Settings > Mini player

![volume ring](https://files.lowtechguys.com/changelog/muskey/1.4.0-volume-ring.png)

## Improvements

- The HUD opens on the first launch
- The HUD keeps its shape: the speed shows as `0.9×` inside the BPM pill, **Play all** is the `0 play all` hint under the instruments, and a taller HUD grows downward
- Other apps can play sound while a slowed song is paused
- Fewer audio cuts on the phone over a slow connection
- The phone says when the Mac doesn't answer, and why a seek or a loop was refused
- **Reset Link** ends the phone's stream, and the phone asks for the new link
- Big folders open faster
- Lower CPU use while a song with a changed key is paused, and while a phone is connected
- Lyrics show **Retry** when Muskey is offline, instead of saying that the song has none
- The pause timeout waits while the notes window is open
- Shortcuts like `⌘W` and `⌘Q`, which every app uses, can't be recorded as Muskey hotkeys
